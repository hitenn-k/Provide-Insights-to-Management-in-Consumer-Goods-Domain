📊 Consumer Goods Domain – SQL Data Analytics Project
📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on providing actionable business insights to management in the Consumer Goods domain using MySQL.

The objective is to solve 10 ad-hoc business requests from AtliQ Hardware by analyzing sales, products, customers, manufacturing costs, and market performance.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Database: MySQL
Language: SQL
IDE: MySQL Workbench
🔍 Key Business Requests
Identify markets where Atliq Exclusive operates in the APAC region.
Calculate the percentage increase in unique products in 2021 vs. 2020.
Analyze unique product counts across different segments.
Identify the segment with the highest product growth in 2021.
Find products with the highest and lowest manufacturing costs.
Identify the top 5 customers receiving the highest average pre-invoice discounts in India for FY 2021.
Generate monthly gross sales reports for Atliq Exclusive.
Identify the quarter with the highest sales quantity in FY 2020.
Analyze sales channel contributions to gross sales in FY 2021.
Identify the top 3 products in each division based on sales quantity in FY 2021.
💡 SQL Concepts Used
Joins
Aggregate Functions
Subqueries
Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
Window Functions
Ranking Functions
GROUP BY and HAVING
Date Functions
Percentage Calculations
📈 Key Learnings
Translating business requirements into SQL queries.
Analyzing sales and product performance.
Understanding customer discount patterns.
Evaluating market and channel performance.
Generating data-driven insights for management decisions.
🎯 Project Outcome
Successfully addressed 10 ad-hoc business requests using MySQL to demonstrate practical SQL skills and business-oriented data analysis.
