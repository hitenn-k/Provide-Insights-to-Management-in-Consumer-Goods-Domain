📊 Consumer Goods Domain – Ad-Hoc Business Insights Using MySQL
📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing business data in the Consumer Goods Domain using MySQL to generate actionable insights for management.

The project addresses 10 ad-hoc business requests from the management team of AtliQ Hardware. Using SQL queries and advanced analytical techniques, the objective is to uncover valuable insights related to product performance, customer behavior, sales trends, market presence, and channel contribution.

The analysis helps management make informed, data-driven decisions and identify opportunities for business growth.

🎯 Business Objectives
Analyze the company's market presence across different geographical regions.
Evaluate product portfolio growth year over year.
Identify high-performing product segments and divisions.
Analyze manufacturing costs and customer discount patterns.
Track monthly and quarterly gross sales performance.
Evaluate sales channel contributions.
Identify top-performing products based on sales quantity.
